# Support VibeRevert

VibeRevert is Apache-2.0 open source. If it's saved you from a bad AI coding session — or you want it to keep getting more trustworthy — you can [sponsor its development](https://github.com/sponsors/madeinplutofabio?metadata_campaign=viberevert).

## What your support funds

- **Cross-platform testing** — keeping the checkpoint and restore engine correct on Linux, macOS, and Windows across supported Node versions.
- **Security work** — hardening the archive and rollback paths, dependency and supply-chain review, and responding to reports.
- **Rollback and recovery testing** — the real dogfood runs and exact-restoration checks behind the [beta report](beta-report.md), extended to more frameworks and scenarios.
- **Independent review** — helping pay for outside eyes on the safety-critical code.

## What it does not buy

Sponsorship does not influence risk findings or release decisions. What VibeRevert reports, how it classifies risk, and when it ships stay independent of every sponsor. Funding pays for the work; it does not shape the verdict.
