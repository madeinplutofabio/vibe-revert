# Trademarks

"VibeRevert" and the official VibeRevert logos are marks controlled by Fabio
Marcello Salvadori. This document explains how you may and may not use them. It
is a brand-use guideline, not legal advice.

## Code license vs. trademark

VibeRevert's source code is licensed under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE). That license
covers copyright in the code; it does **not** grant any right to use the
VibeRevert name, logos, or other marks. As Apache-2.0 states, the license does
not grant permission to use the licensor's trade names or trademarks except for
reasonable and customary use in describing the origin of the work.

In other words: you are free to use, modify, and redistribute the code under
Apache-2.0, but that freedom does not extend to the VibeRevert brand.

## What you may do

Truthful references that identify VibeRevert or describe compatibility are
generally permitted under this guideline:

- State that your project uses, works with, integrates with, or is compatible
  with VibeRevert.
- Refer to VibeRevert by name in documentation, articles, talks, and comparisons.
- Redistribute unmodified VibeRevert with its name and notices intact.
- State that a fork or modified build is based on VibeRevert, provided its own
  name and branding are clearly distinct and the reference does not imply
  endorsement.

## What to avoid

- Do not use the VibeRevert name or official logos in a way likely to make users
  believe that your product, service, domain, or modified distribution is
  official, endorsed by, or affiliated with VibeRevert.
- Do not apply the VibeRevert name to a modified or forked version in a way that
  suggests it is the official VibeRevert.
- Do not use or modify an official VibeRevert logo as branding for another
  product or service without permission.

## Commercial and hosted offerings

Apache-2.0 permits commercial use of the code; the trademark is handled
separately. Reserved product and namespace boundaries — including any hosted or
commercial offering — are described in the
[commercial boundary](docs/commercial-boundary.md). You may truthfully state that
a service uses or supports VibeRevert; do not brand a hosted or commercial
offering in a way likely to imply that it is the official VibeRevert service or is
endorsed by the VibeRevert maintainer.

## Questions

For any use beyond truthful reference that identifies VibeRevert — including logo
use or any use that could imply affiliation — please ask the maintainer first.
